This Privacy Policy describes how MindMaze (“MindMaze,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the MindMaze mobile application and related services (the “Service”).

MindMaze is an anonymous-style social app for sharing Thoughts and Wishes. Other people may see the content you post together with an Anonymous ID. That is not a guarantee of complete anonymity.

1. Who we are

MindMaze is operated by an independent developer. For privacy questions, contact mindmaze.support@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Google Sign-In and authentication

You sign in with Google through Supabase Auth. We do not operate a separate username-and-password account system.

Supabase Auth stores Google-derived account metadata associated with your sign-in. That metadata currently includes email, name, full_name, avatar_url, picture, and Google’s user identifier (sub), together with the Supabase Auth user ID assigned to your account.

The MindMaze app uses the Supabase Auth UUID as your account identifier inside the Service (including to load your profile and as the RevenueCat App User ID). The app does not copy your Google name, email, or profile photo into MindMaze profile records, and it does not show those Google details to other users on posts or public profile surfaces.

Session tokens issued by Supabase are stored on your device so you can stay signed in. MindMaze does not store Google access tokens or Google refresh tokens in its own application database.

2.2 Profile information you provide

After sign-in, you create a MindMaze profile. We store information such as:

  • Anonymous ID (display_id), which other users may see
  • Gender, used for Wish matching
  • Date of birth
  • Timezone, used for local daily posting limits
  • Account status (for example, active, suspended, or banned)

Google name, email, and avatar are not copied into this profile record.

MindMaze does not currently impose its own 18+ registration restriction in the app. Date of birth is collected as profile information and is not used as an in-app age gate at this time.

2.3 Content and activity

When you use the Service, we process:

  • Thoughts and Wishes you post (text, mode, and related metadata)
  • Delivery records when a post is shown to you (views)
  • Ratings you submit on posts you were shown
  • Reports you submit, including reason and optional detail
  • Blocks you create or that affect Explore visibility

Other users who receive your post may see the post text and your Anonymous ID. They do not see your Google name, email, or profile photo through MindMaze.

2.4 Purchases

Optional MindMaze Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through RevenueCat. We use your Supabase Auth UUID as the RevenueCat App User ID. MindMaze does not store full payment card numbers. Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and entitlement information according to their own policies.

2.5 Safety and moderation

Posts may be checked by an automated Safety Filter. Some content is blocked from being posted. Other content may be flagged for internal review. A flag or a user report does not by itself automatically punish an account. Human moderation may hide a post or suspend or ban an account. We keep internal safety flags, reports, and operator audit records as needed to review and act on harm.

2.6 Device information

We read the IANA timezone from your device and store it on your profile so daily posting limits follow your local calendar day. MindMaze does not currently use location data for advertising. This section describes current behavior and is not a promise about future features.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Operate posting, Explore, rating, History, Status, and blocking
  • Match Wishes using gender information as implemented in the Service
  • Provide Premium features when your purchase is active
  • Review reports and safety flags, and take human moderation actions
  • Maintain the Service, prevent abuse, and comply with law

4. How we share information

We share information in these ways:

  • Other users. Post text and Anonymous ID may be shown to people who receive your posts. Ratings are not shown as another person’s identity.
  • Service providers. We use service providers such as Google, Supabase, Apple, and RevenueCat to provide authentication, backend infrastructure, App Store purchases, and subscription management. Google is used for Sign-In. Supabase is used for authentication and application data. Apple processes App Store purchases. RevenueCat is used for subscription and entitlement management, with your Supabase Auth UUID as the RevenueCat App User ID.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or to address serious safety issues.

5. Anonymity

MindMaze is designed so that other users see an Anonymous ID rather than your Google name or email. You should not treat that as complete anonymity. Your Anonymous ID can appear next to content you post. You should not include information in a post that you do not want others to see.

6. Account deletion

You can delete your MindMaze account from inside the app. Account deletion deletes related MindMaze account and content data associated with that account, subject to the following:

  • Some records needed for system integrity (for example, ratings left on other people’s posts, or certain billing/event records) may be retained in anonymized form.
  • Deleting your MindMaze account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Manage or cancel Premium through your Apple ID subscription settings.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate MindMaze, maintain safety and integrity, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve disputes. Different categories of information may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose. After account deletion, some records needed for system integrity, safety or legal obligations, or subscription processing by Apple or RevenueCat may remain in anonymized or provider-held form, as described in Account deletion.

8. Children

MindMaze currently does not impose its own 18+ registration restriction. Date of birth is collected during profile setup, but the app does not currently use it as an in-app age gate. Users must comply with applicable laws, platform requirements, and the Terms of Use. Age-related requirements may be changed where necessary.

9. International processing

MindMaze uses third-party service providers that may process or store information in countries or regions other than where you live. Their processing is subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable law.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service, our data practices, or applicable requirements change. When we do, we will update the Last Updated date on this page. We may also provide additional notice where we consider it appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions about this policy, email mindmaze.support@gmail.com.
