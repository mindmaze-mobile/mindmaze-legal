MindMaze Premium

Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s App Store. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple subscription settings. Deleting a MindMaze account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Restore Purchases can be used in MindMaze where applicable.

Account deletion

You can delete your MindMaze account from inside the app. Deletion is permanent. Cancelling an App Store subscription is a separate step in your Apple settings.

Safety

If you think a post violates the Community Guidelines, you can report it in the app. If you no longer want to see someone’s posts, you can block them.

MindMaze Support is not an emergency service and is not a substitute for contacting local emergency services or the police if you are in immediate danger.
