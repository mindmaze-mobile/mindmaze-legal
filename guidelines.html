MindMaze is a place for thoughts people rarely say out loud.

You may encounter opinions, feelings, or wishes that you disagree with or find uncomfortable. That is part of exploring what other people really think.

We want MindMaze to remain open enough for honest thoughts while still being a safe and human place: a space where inconvenient honesty can appear, and where people’s humanity is still protected.

Be honest. Be human. Keep it safe.

The boundary is harm — not discomfort.

These Guidelines sit alongside the Terms of Use. Using MindMaze means following both.

1. Be honest

Honest Thoughts and Wishes are welcome. An unpopular or uncomfortable opinion is not automatically a violation. Disagreement itself is not abuse.

Post your own genuine thoughts. Do not impersonate someone else. Do not use MindMaze for spam, scams, or manipulation.

2. Be human

There is a person on the other side of every post. Personal taste and candid preference are not the same as attacking someone.

For example, saying that you are not personally attracted to a certain body type is a private preference. Someone else may find that uncomfortable. Discomfort alone does not make it a violation.

Directing degrading abuse, harassment, or threats at a person or a group is not allowed.

3. Sexual topics

Sexual topics are not prohibited simply because they are sexual.

You may speak plainly about attraction, dating, relationships, intimacy, bodies, sexual preferences, and what you wish for in relationships.

The following are not allowed:

  • Sexual harassment
  • Sexual exploitation
  • Non-consensual sexual content or conduct
  • Threats of sexual violence
  • Sexualisation or sexual exploitation of minors

4. Keep it safe

Do not:

  • Harass others or direct targeted degrading abuse at them
  • Make credible threats or encourage violence
  • Make hateful or degrading attacks against protected groups
  • Share another person’s private or identifying information without permission
  • Engage in sexual exploitation
  • Sexualisation or sexual exploitation of minors
  • Post or seek non-consensual sexual content or conduct
  • Use the Service for serious illegal or dangerous activity
  • Run scams or spam
  • Try to evade or abuse safety or moderation systems

5. Personal information

MindMaze is not a promise of complete anonymity. Other people may see your Anonymous ID next to what you post.

Do not put the following in a post if you can avoid it: full name, email, phone number, address, social media contact information, or other details that could identify you or someone else. An automated Safety Filter may reject some contact details and similar information before a post is created.

Do not post another person’s personal information without permission.

6. Report vs Block

Report a post when you believe it crosses these Guidelines and may be harmful or unsafe. Reports help us review possible harm.

Block a person when you do not want to see their posts anymore. A block does not have to mean they broke the Guidelines. It only hides them from your Explore path.

Disagreement by itself is usually a reason to keep scrolling, or to block, rather than to report. If something feels unsafe or harmful, you can still report it. We would rather hear from you than have you stay silent about harm.

7. Moderation

MindMaze may use automated safety checks, user reports, and human review. The Safety Filter may reject some posts before they are created, or flag posts for internal review. A flag is not an automatic finding that someone broke these Guidelines.

After review, we may take no action, hide or remove content, suspend an account, or ban an account. We do not guarantee that every post is reviewed in advance.

Say what you really think.
Just remember that everyone else in the maze is human too.
